How to use this system
  1. Choose a demonstration role above. Every action is re-checked on the server for that role — hidden buttons are not the only protection.
  2. Enter transactions under New entry: Richard, Anastasia and Jean-Claude enter sales with a proposed commission split (Richard / Anastasia / Jean-Claude); Kevin enters expenses with a proposed allocation.
  3. Or use Telegram: open the bot, press Start, and ask Svetlana (Manager setup below) to link your Telegram user ID to an employee. Then send for example
    /sale S01 | Olivia Rose | A | One proud uncle and an emotional grandmother | 1000 | 50/30/20
    /expense E01 | Rented suit and fake pearl necklace for the relatives | Materials | 120 | A
  4. Approve decisions as Svetlana under Manager decisions: keep or change the commission split and approve a sale; confirm or change an expense allocation. The submitter is notified on Telegram.
  5. Results (Svetlana's view) update immediately. Records are copied automatically to the Google Sheet (tabs Sales and Expenses); if a copy or a notification fails, the record shows it with a Retry button.

Link Telegram users to employees

Each person first opens the bot and presses Start; the bot replies with their Telegram user ID and they appear here. Bot submissions keep the chat they came from for later notifications, even if the link changes afterwards.

Integrations
